## 3. Frequently asked questions and approved answers

### Q: What is PunterStat?
**A:** PunterStat is a data-driven sports analysis, education and betting-assistance platform. It combines match analysis, learning resources and tools such as data-driven picks, a bet-slip checker, calculators and a bet tracker. It helps users understand the numbers behind sports and betting decisions.

### Q: Is PunterStat a bookmaker?
**A:** No. PunterStat is not a bookmaker or betting exchange. It does not accept bets, stakes or betting deposits and does not hold betting funds. It provides analysis, education and tools. Any bet is placed separately by the customer with a third-party operator.

### Q: Does PunterStat provide betting tips?
**A:** PunterStat's Bet Assist area includes data-driven picks based on model probabilities, market prices and value indicators. These are statistical estimates, not guaranteed tips or sure wins. Users should independently assess the information and make their own decisions.

### Q: Who can use PunterStat?
**A:** The platform's betting tools are intended for people aged 18 or older, or the legal gambling age in their jurisdiction if higher. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of betting-related tools is lawful where they live.

### Q: Do I need to install an app?
**A:** No installation is required to use the website. PunterStat is a web platform accessible through a modern browser on a phone, tablet or desktop.

### Q: Does PunterStat work on mobile?
**A:** Yes. The website is responsive and is designed to work on modern mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.

## 4. Platform modules

### 4.1 Bet Analysis

**Match Breakdown Engine**
- Provides an educational breakdown of a match using six factors: recent form, head-to-head record, goal-scoring and expected-goals data, home advantage, injury/suspension impact and match stakes.
- Displays probability estimates and explanatory information, including probability bars, implied odds and expected goals.
- The model output is an estimate, not a prediction guarantee.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/match-breakdown

**Spot The Value**
- Allows users to compare their own probability estimates with available market odds.
- The page describes cached odds refreshed approximately every five minutes.
- It explains bookmaker overround, fair implied probability and the difference between a model's probability and the market's fair probability.
- A value gap is not proof that a bet will win.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/spot-the-value

**Simulation Engine**
- Includes a Bet Simulator and a Probability Simulator.
- The Bet Simulator starts with a virtual ₦10,000 balance and lets users explore outcomes, streaks, ROI and simulated profit/loss.
- The Probability Simulator uses Monte Carlo simulations to illustrate possible long-run outcomes and variance.
- Simulations use virtual currency only; they do not place real bets or move real money.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/simulation-engine

**Results Browser and Head-to-Head**
- These are listed as Bet Analysis resources in the website navigation.
- If a customer asks about a specific result, data source, supported competition or exact refresh schedule not covered here, do not guess. Ask for the match/competition and escalate or direct the customer to the relevant page.

### 4.2 Bet Learning

**Betting Academy**
- Teaches betting mathematics and literacy, including odds formats, implied probability, expected value, variance, Kelly Criterion, bankroll management and risk.
- The public FAQ and pricing page describe the core Betting Academy lessons as free.

**Sports University**
- Structured educational content about how sports work, including football tactics, formations, pressing systems, squad rotation, home advantage and tournament structures.
- The public FAQ and pricing page describe Sports University lessons as free.

**Other learning resources**
- Betting Markets Glossary: explanations of markets such as 1X2, Asian handicap, over/under, both teams to score and draw-no-bet.
- League Glossary: information about competitions, formats and teams.
- Blog: articles and educational guides.

### 4.3 Bet Assist

**Data-Driven Picks**
- The website describes model probabilities for upcoming fixtures, based on bookmaker consensus and team form, with value flags where an available price is better than the model's fair odds.
- Picks and prices can change. Do not state a pick, price or availability as current unless verified on the live page.
- Never guarantee an outcome.

**Bet Slip Checker**
- Lets a user enter the legs of a single or accumulator bet, selections, decimal odds and optionally their own estimated chance.
- It explains what the combined price implies and highlights margin or issues worth considering.
- It is an analysis tool; it does not submit the slip to a bookmaker or place a bet.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/bet-assist/slip-checker

**Betting Calculators**
The public calculator page lists:
- Expected Value (EV) and Kelly stake.
- Accumulator calculations.
- Bookmaker margin and fair odds.
- Odds conversion.
- Arbitrage calculations.

The Kelly tool uses the user's odds, estimated probability and bankroll inputs. Kelly outputs are mathematical estimates, not instructions or guarantees. Full Kelly can be aggressive and depends on the accuracy of the probability estimate.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/bet-assist/calculators

**Bet Tracker**
- The website describes a tracker for recording bets and reviewing performance measures such as profit, ROI, strike rate, closing-line value and bankroll history.
- Do not claim that PunterStat can import a customer's bookmaker account or retrieve bookmaker transactions unless this is verified in the live product.
- If a tracker is missing, inaccessible or not saving entries, collect the error and escalate.

## 5. Accounts, registration and access

### Q: Is registration free?
**A:** Yes. Creating a PunterStat account is free. The public pricing page offers a Free plan, and the educational Sports University and Betting Academy content is described as free.

### Q: I cannot sign in. What should I do?
**A:** Check that you are using the email address associated with your account and that there are no typing errors. Refresh the page and try again. If the issue continues, use the website's available password-reset process. If you still cannot access the account, email support@punterstat.site with the account email and a description of the issue. Never send your password or one-time passcode.

### Q: I did not receive an account or password-reset email.
**A:** Check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. Wait a few minutes before requesting another email. If it still does not arrive, contact support@punterstat.site and include the account email and approximate time of the request. Do not share passwords or verification codes.

### Q: Can I use one account on my phone and computer?
**A:** PunterStat is described as a responsive web platform for modern browsers. If you encounter a session or access problem when switching devices, contact support with the browser, device type and error message. Do not share login credentials.

### Q: Can I get group or institutional access?
**A:** The FAQ says group or institutional access can be discussed for sports academies, coaching staff, universities and corporate groups. Contact hello@punterstat.site for details and pricing.

## 7. Technical troubleshooting

Use these low-risk steps for general website problems:
1. Refresh the page.
2. Confirm the device has a working internet connection.
3. Open the website in an up-to-date version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.
4. Try a private/incognito window to check whether a browser extension or cached session is involved.
5. If appropriate, clear the browser cache for the site and sign in again.
6. Try another modern browser or device to determine whether the issue is device-specific.
7. Record the exact error message, page URL, device, browser and approximate time.

Do not ask a customer to disable security protections, install unknown software, share remote access or reveal credentials.

### Q: The website is not loading.
**A:** Please check your internet connection, refresh the page and try an up-to-date browser. If the problem continues, try another browser or device and send support@punterstat.site the page URL, error message, browser and device details.

### Q: A tool is blank, stuck or not calculating.
**A:** Refresh the page and check that all required fields are completed with valid values. If it still does not work, send support the name of the tool, the page URL, the steps you took, the error message and a screenshot with private information hidden.

### Q: My progress, bookmarks or simulation history disappeared.
**A:** Confirm that you are signed into the same account used previously. Refresh and sign in again. If the information is still missing, contact support@punterstat.site with the account email, affected feature and approximate last time the data was visible. Do not promise data recovery.

### Q: The odds or fixture information looks outdated.
**A:** Market odds and fixture information can change. The Spot The Value page says its displayed odds are cached and refreshed approximately every five minutes. Check the live page and the bookmaker directly before making any decision. If the information appears incorrect, send support the fixture, market, displayed price and time observed.

### Q: A match or market is missing.
**A:** Coverage can vary by competition, fixture and market. Ask for the sport, competition, teams, event date and market. Do not promise that a particular event or market is supported. Escalate if the customer believes a normally available item has disappeared.

### Q: Does PunterStat sell my personal information?
**A:** PunterStat's Privacy Policy says it does not sell personal data. For further details, see https://punterstat.site/privacy.

### Q: How do I request a copy of my data or correct it?

Support chat should not claim deletion is complete until the privacy team confirms it.

### Q: Does PunterStat use cookies?
**A:** The Privacy Policy says it uses strictly necessary cookies to maintain an authenticated session and does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. Disabling session cookies may require you to sign in on every visit.

## 9. Responsible use and gambling safety

PunterStat's public guidance says:
- Use betting tools only if you are at least 18 or meet the higher legal age where you live.
- Only use licensed betting operators where you live.
- Treat betting as entertainment, not as a way to earn income or pay debts.
- Set a bankroll you can afford to lose and do not use money needed for bills or savings.
- Do not chase losses or increase stakes to recover money.
- Take a break if betting stops being enjoyable or begins affecting wellbeing.

The Responsible Gambling page lists support resources including Gambling Therapy, BeGambleAware, Gamblers Anonymous and Gamban.
- Page: https://punterstat.site/responsible-gambling

### Q: Can PunterStat guarantee that I will win?
**A:** No. PunterStat's probabilities and picks are estimates based on data and models. No result is guaranteed, and even a well-reasoned decision can lose.

### Q: I lost money following a pick. Can PunterStat refund my betting losses?
**A:** PunterStat does not take bets or hold betting funds. Wagers are placed with third-party operators, and PunterStat cannot refund or reverse those wagers. If your concern is about a PunterStat subscription payment, contact support@punterstat.site. If betting is causing distress, consider taking a break and using the support resources on the Responsible Gambling page.

### Q: I cannot stop betting or I am chasing losses.
**A:** I'm sorry you're dealing with that. Please pause betting and avoid trying to win losses back. PunterStat is not a gambling operator or crisis-support service. The Responsible Gambling page lists free support resources, including Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone you trust.

### Q: Can PunterStat access or change my bookmaker account?
**A:** No. PunterStat does not control third-party bookmaker accounts, balances, deposits, withdrawals or wagers. Contact the bookmaker directly about those matters.

## 12. Known limitations and facts the AI must not invent

The public website does not provide enough verified information in this research to answer every possible operational question. Escalate rather than inventing:
- Exact account recovery steps beyond the visible password-reset flow.
- Manual account activation or identity-verification procedures.
- Exact refund exceptions or authority to approve refunds.
- Payment settlement timelines, chargeback procedures or payment-provider outage status.
- Guaranteed odds-feed coverage, all supported bookmakers or all supported sports/competitions.
- Exact data refresh schedules for every feature.
- Exact bet-tracker import/export or bookmaker integration capabilities.
- Guaranteed prediction accuracy, winning rates or financial returns.
- A customer's subscription status, payment status, saved data or account history.
- A guaranteed support resolution time.

### Public-page conflicts to monitor
1. Free-plan feature entitlements conflict between the pricing page and FAQ, as described in Section 6.
2. The FAQ says it does not collect financial data and that payment details are not stored directly, while the Privacy Policy says it stores subscription plan, status and payment reference. Use the more precise Privacy Policy wording: third-party payment processing, with plan/status/reference stored by PunterStat, not full card details.
3. Some feature pages may change or be unavailable. Always defer to the live website and an authorised support agent for current operational status.
